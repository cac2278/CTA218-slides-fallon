SCHOLARLY SOURCES IN MEDIA & CULTURAL STUDIES

How do you identify peer-reviewed research?

Peer-review process in media, film, and cultural studies: First, authors submit a manuscript to a journal. Second, an editor screens it for scope and suitability. Third, specialists assess the research methods, evidence, and conclusions. Fourth, the editor decides whether to accept, request revision and resubmission, or reject. After revision, the editor may accept or reject the manuscript. Accepted work is published. Peer review improves scrutiny but does not guarantee correctness.
